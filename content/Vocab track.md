Made to keep a track of all the new words I learn and use.

- [[urban tree health mapping app#How I got this idea | Arborist]] - A specialist in the care of woody plants, especially trees. 
- `Melodramatic` - Exaggeratedly emotional or sentimental; histrionic. 
- `Histrionic` - A drama performer; a stage-player. The histrionics of a stump-speaker.
- `Lusophone` - Refers to the Portuguese speaking world, including the people, cultures, and countries where Portuguese is spoken. It combines the prefix _"Luso-"_ (from _Lusitania_, the Roman name for the region of modern Portugal) and _"-phone"_ (from the Greek for voice or language).
