### What the project is about ??
It is a data pipeline for AI assisted marketing. That being said let us go into some of the details. 

So, I will give a brief idea of how things work or will work as I am still working on the project. I would like to point out that over here you will find only a overview of the project. 
- The user give input in the form of a text, image, video, `3D` model, etc. This inputs are processed. 
- A market research agent understands the product and does research on trends in the space of that product
- A image generation agent produces an image using the research of the market research agent. 

This gives you a gist of the project. The pipeline is way more detailed then this, but this gives you a idea. 

### Why this
Using a standard, direct-use image generation AI (like Midjourney or ChatGPT with DALL-E) is like having a talented painter but no art director. You have to meticulously prompt it, hope it understands the vibe, and try to force it not to distort your actual product.

You aren't just generating one image at a time. The pipeline offers distinct execution modes depending on your business goal:
- **E-Commerce Batch:** Bypasses research to rapidly generate 5–12 clean, studio-style catalog images in one go.

- **A/B Concept Exploration:** Intentionally generates 3–4 radically different visual aesthetics (e.g., Minimalist vs. Maximalist) to test brand positioning.

- **Seasonal Campaign:** Automatically injects seasonal props and lighting (e.g., Christmas or Summer vibes) into the prompts.

Normal AI forces the user to become a prompt engineer. This pipeline abstracts that away entirely. You provide structured inputs (Product Info, Target Audience, Desired Colors) and steering parameters (Aspect Ratio, Lighting Presets). The **Vision Agent** analyzes your uploads, and the **Image Designer Agent** constructs the highly complex generation prompt for you under the hood.
